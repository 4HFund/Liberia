Our journey · October 2026

More than a trip. A story of people.

Pastor Bobby Westfall and Sidney Mozingo traveled to Liberia at the invitation of Free Methodist World Missions to listen, learn, and document the faith and vision of the people they met.

In churches, classrooms, and communities, we met people leading, teaching, worshiping, and building their own futures. We came to listen and share what we learned.

People leading the way

Leadership rooted in Liberia.

The Free Methodist Church in Liberia is guided by leaders who know their communities and are shaping the ministry already taking place there. Our October 2026 visit was an opportunity to listen, learn, and strengthen relationships.

The relationships and vision of Liberian church leaders are central to this story. The Foundery's role is to listen, document, and encourage locally led ministry.

A journey beyond the road

The road ended.

Beyond the forest trail, we found a community sharing its faith, educating its children in the church while a new school remains unfinished, and imagining what the future could grow.

Follow the forest trail into Zuzhon and meet the people who welcomed us.

The vision behind the name

100 acres.
Room to grow.

The proposed 100-acre agricultural initiative near Zuzhon envisions a future where locally led farming, livelihoods, education, and ministry can strengthen one another.

This is a developing vision, not a completed farm or a promise of specific outcomes. Its direction depends on Liberian leadership, planning, and partners.
