Meet the team

Bobby Alan Westfall

Lead PastorMissionaryStoryteller

Bobby Alan Westfall is the Lead Pastor of The Foundery Church in Wellsburg, West Virginia. Known for his passionate teaching and pastoral heart, Bobby, along with his wife Stacy and their five children, leads the church in cultivating authentic worship, intentional community, and missional living.

Bobby's journey began with a life marked by hopelessness and wild living until a transformative encounter with Jesus on November 14, 2000. He spent 12 years sharing his testimony as a hip-hop artist with the group Kalledout, then traveled with Stacy as missionaries on The Extreme Tour, reaching at-risk youth through music and extreme sports. Together, they co-founded The Bay Six Project, a ministry devoted to making sure everyone is seen, loved, and valued.

Today, Bobby and his family are committed to bringing hope, restoration, and the gospel to the Ohio Valley, pointing people to Jesus, the unshakable center of everything they do, one conversation at a time.
Meet the team

Sidney Lee Mozingo

Army VeteranCreative StorytellerMinistry Leader

Sidney Lee Mozingo is a U.S. Army veteran, creative storyteller, and ministry leader serving at The Foundery Church in Wellsburg, West Virginia. Alongside his wife, Jacy, and their family, Sidney uses media, design, film, and technology to help people feel seen, valued, and connected.

After serving in Iraq and Afghanistan, Sidney's journey led him through seasons of struggle, growth, and a renewed sense of purpose. Today, he uses his experiences and creativity to share meaningful stories, strengthen the local church, and bring attention to work that is making a real difference.

Whether behind a camera, building a new idea, or serving his community, Sidney is passionate about using creativity for something greater than himself and helping others see the hope God is growing around them.
