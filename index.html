The heart of the mission

The story is already alive.

The faith, leadership, relationships, and vision are already present in Liberia. This mission is about crossing the distance, listening carefully, and creating a film that lets those voices lead.

“We are not going to become the center of the story. We are going to help the story travel farther.”
Chapter 01 · Listen First

Meet people before framing the story.

Arrival begins with relationships. Local pastors, families, and ministry partners help shape what the cameras should follow and why it matters.

Chapter 02 · Faith in Motion

Joy is part of the story.

Worship, celebration, movement, and everyday community life reveal more than an interview ever could. The documentary will make room for those moments to speak for themselves.

Chapter 03 · Local Churches

Faith keeps gathering while the walls are still rising.

Pastors preach, families worship, and leaders serve while church spaces continue to grow around them. This is living faith, not a future promise.

Chapter 04 · The Vision in the Ground

One hundred acres can grow more than crops.

The long-term vision brings cacao, animals, food production, training, ministry, and employment together. The land is a starting point for opportunity that can keep growing.

100 acres Cacao Animals Training Opportunity
Chapter 05 · Bring the Story Home

See why the journey matters.

The finished documentary, photographs, interviews, and short videos will help churches and partners see the work, pray with understanding, and support the vision for years to come.

The team behind the camera

Servants, listeners, and storytellers.

Pastor Bobby and Sidney are not traveling to become the heroes. Their job is to build trust, listen well, and create the space for Liberian leaders to tell the story in their own voice.

Trust and transparency

Clear answers matter.

Global partnership requires responsible stewardship, realistic planning, and respect for every person whose life and ministry appears on screen.

Church-administered givingDonations are processed and administered through The Foundery Church.
Liberian voices remain centralThe film is designed around local leaders, churches, families, workers, and communities.
Media made for long-term useThe documentary and supporting content can continue serving the mission after the trip.
Where will the money go?

Funds support approved mission and documentary expenses such as international airfare, transportation, lodging, meals, travel requirements, insurance, media storage, and production needs.

How is travel safety being handled?

The team will follow the guidance of host church leaders, current travel requirements, insurance requirements, and real-time local conditions.

Does sponsoring an acre mean I own land?

No. The $60 acre sponsorship is symbolic. It does not create ownership, equity, or any legal interest in property in Liberia.

Whose story will the documentary center?

Liberian pastors, church leaders, families, workers, and community members will remain at the center.

What will be created after the trip?

The primary deliverable is a modern documentary, along with photographs, interviews, presentation materials, and short videos for awareness, prayer, partnership, and future support.
