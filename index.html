The People Behind the Camera

Not the heroes. The bridge.

Pastor Bobby and Sidney are not traveling to speak over the Liberian church. They are going to listen carefully, build trust, and help local pastors, families, workers, and leaders share what God is already doing in their own words.

BW
Pastor · Listener · Interviewer

Pastor Bobby Westfall

Pastor Bobby serves The Foundery Church with a heart for practical ministry and meaningful partnership. In Liberia, his role will be to build relationships, ask thoughtful questions, and create space for local church leaders to tell the story in their own voice.

SM
Army Veteran · Filmmaker · Storyteller

Sidney Mozingo

Sidney serves in creative and media ministry at The Foundery. He will lead documentary production, photography, audio, and post-production with a commitment to context, dignity, and visual storytelling that keeps Liberian people—not the filmmakers—at the center.
