A journey worth sharing

We came to listen. We left with so much more.

In October 2026, Pastor Bobby Westfall and Sidney Mozingo traveled to Liberia with an invitation from Free Methodist World Missions: to listen, learn, and document the faith, leadership, and vision of local communities.

From worship services and classrooms to villages and agricultural land, we witnessed relationships and possibilities that deserve to be shared with care. The trip is over. The relationships and the story continue.

People before projects

Hope isn't something we brought to Liberia. It's something we witnessed growing there.

This story belongs first to the Liberian pastors, families, educators, and community leaders building their future.

The vision behind the name

100 acres.
Room to grow.

The vision for a 100-acre agricultural initiative in Liberia is about more than land. It is about exploring ways that local leadership, sustainable livelihoods, education, and ministry can grow together.

The ideas below represent aspirations and development work, not completed or guaranteed outcomes.
