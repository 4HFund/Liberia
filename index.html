Meet the team

Bobby Alan Westfall

Lead PastorMissionaryStoryteller

Bobby Alan Westfall is the Lead Pastor of The Foundery Church in Wellsburg, West Virginia. Known for his passionate teaching and pastoral heart, Bobby, along with his wife Stacy and their five children, leads the church in cultivating authentic worship, intentional community, and missional living.

Bobby spent 12 years sharing his testimony as a hip-hop artist with Kalledout, then traveled with Stacy as missionaries on The Extreme Tour. Together, they co-founded The Bay Six Project, a ministry devoted to making sure everyone is seen, loved, and valued.

Today, Bobby and his family are committed to bringing hope, restoration, and the gospel to the Ohio Valley.
Meet the team

Sidney Lee Mozingo

Army VeteranCreative StorytellerMinistry Leader

Sidney Lee Mozingo is a U.S. Army veteran, creative storyteller, and ministry leader serving at The Foundery Church in Wellsburg, West Virginia. Alongside his wife, Jacy, and their family, Sidney uses media, design, film, and technology to help people feel seen, valued, and connected.

After serving in Iraq and Afghanistan, Sidney's journey led him through seasons of struggle, growth, and renewed purpose. Today, he uses creativity to strengthen the local church and bring attention to work making a real difference.

Whether behind a camera or building a new idea, Sidney is passionate about using creativity for something greater than himself.

Questions

Quick answers.

What is 100 Acres of Hope?

It is a documentary mission sharing the faith, people, and long-term vision growing across 100 acres in Liberia.

Why are you traveling to Liberia?

We are going to listen, build relationships, document the work, and help Liberian leaders share their story.

What will donations help cover?

Gifts help with travel, transportation, lodging, documentary production, and sharing the finished story.

Can I donate anonymously?

Yes. Anonymous giving options are available through the secure donation page.

Can I help without donating?

Yes. Sharing the campaign, praying, encouraging the team, or connecting us with a community partner all help.

When does the trip begin?

Our Liberia journey begins October 16. We plan to share updates and livestream during the trip whenever internet connection allows.
